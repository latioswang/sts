### Immolate `attack` `rare` `cost 2` `tier S-`

_tags_: pollute, sacrifice

**Effect.** Deal 21 damage to ALL enemies. Shuffle a Burn into your discard pile.

**Upgrade.** Deal 28 damage to ALL enemies. Shuffle a Burn into your discard pile.

**Notes.** Immolate is a really powerful attack for hallway fights. It has very high aoe damage and is capable of killing multiple foes in one turn. the burn card goes into your discard pile so in many fights you are unlikely to even draw it before the encounter is over. ## Tier A+

### Corruption `power` `rare` `cost 3→2` `tier A+`

_tags_: exhaustother

**Effect.** Skills cost 0. Whenever you play a Skill, Exhaust it.

**Notes.** This card is an exceptionally powerful win condition, but it is also a risky strategy. Corruption is powerful on the first time through your deck but once your deck is reshuffled and all of your defensive capabilities have been exhausted the fights can become scary. This is why it is very important to be careful how early you cast corruption, particularly in fights that you expect to be long such as boss battles. This card in combination with dead branch is one of the most insane combos in the entire game, as each skill that you exhaust has a chance of generating another free skill and so on and so forth. I typically recommend running one or two Iron Waves in this deck just in case a fight just go longer than you expect it to and you still need a source of block. Barricade can also work very well with corruption as the block that you accumulate does not expire right away. Dark Embrace can lead to cycling your first deck shuffle very quickly too, for better or for worse, and Feel No Pain amps up the block gain.

**★ Super-synergies.** Burning Pact, Corruption, Dark Embrace, Feel No Pain, Sentinel, Dead Branch

**Synergies (31).** Barricade, Dual Wield, Exhume, Flame Barrier, Impervious, Power Through, Rampage, Shockwave, Transmutation, Chrysalis, Metamorphosis, The Bomb, Decay, Doubt, Injury, Normality, Pain, Parasite, Writhe, Frozen Egg, Letter Opener, Mummified Hand, Bird-Faced Urn, Calipers, Gambling Chip, …

**Anti-synergies.** Mayhem, Regret, Pride, Unceasing Top

### Offering `skill` `rare` `cost 0` `tier A+`

_tags_: exhaust, sacrifice, draw, managain

**Effect.** Lose 6 HP. Gain [energy] [energy]. Draw 3 cards. Exhaust.

**Upgrade.** Lose 6 HP. Gain [energy] [energy]. Draw 5 cards. Exhaust.

**Notes.** Offering is a card with a very powerful effect but a pretty huge downside. Although it provides a lot of options through its additional draw and energy gain, it completely negates the healing that you get from your relic. As long as the effect is preventing you from taking six damage in the long room, for example by providing additional energy for block or by ensuring a monster is dead before it deals a high damaging attack, then you are using this card effectively. I would generally recommend having at least one additional source of healing to make this card worth the risk. It is possible to abstain from playing it uncertain fights that are low threat, but this usually feels terrible as ironclad doesn't typically have many options for drawing anyway. 

**★ Super-synergies.** Blood for Blood

**Synergies (57).** Armaments, Dark Embrace, Entrench, Evolve, Exhume, Feed, Feel No Pain, Fire Breathing, Headbutt, Rampage, Reaper, Rupture, Bandage Up, Bite, Panache, Chrysalis, Metamorphosis, Beta, Blood Vial, Happy Flower, Juzu Bracelet, Regal Pillow, Singing Bowl, Strawberry, Bottled Lightning, …

_tags_: draw, drawblock

**Effect.** Draw 3 cards. You cannot draw additional cards this turn.

### Reaper `attack` `rare` `cost 2` `tier A`

_tags_: heal

**Effect.** Deal 4 damage to ALL enemies. Heal for unblocked damage. Exhaust.

**Upgrade.** Deal 5 damage to ALL enemies. Heal for unblocked damage. Exhaust.

**Notes.** Reaper is an incredibly powerful card, and possibly one of ironclad’s strongest. Without any support, the healing that it does is fairly negligible, however its damage and healing is increased by vulnerable and as soon as you start stacking strength this card becomes incredibly strong. It's possible to heal upwards of 50 health in one combat in the right situation. It is not always correct to play repair on the turn that you draw it - sometimes you want to wait for more procs of Demon Form or your Limit Break. Keep in mind that Reaper can only heal for damage done - overkill does not heal you for the excess amount. 

**★ Super-synergies.** Demon Form, Double Tap, Exhume, Thunderclap

**Synergies (43).** Bash, Blood for Blood, Bloodletting, Brutality, Combust, Dropkick, Flex, Heavy Blade, Hemokinesis, Immolate, Inflame, Limit Break, Offering, Rupture, Shockwave, Spot Weakness, Uppercut, J.A.X., Madness, Trip, Decay, Normality, Pain, Bag of Marbles, Pen Nib, …

### Double Tap `skill` `rare` `cost 1` `tier B`

**Effect.** This turn, your next Attack is played twice.

**Upgrade.** This turn, your next 2 Attacks are played twice.

**Notes.** Double tap is typically much more effective on expensive attacks, since the second time it is played it is played for free. Obvious choices are bludgeon, searing blow, and carnage.
