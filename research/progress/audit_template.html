Arriving at the act-2 boss (floor 33)

Runs that died there compared with runs that went on to win. Averages over the traced sample; correlations, not causes — winning runs also see more floors and shops.

What the logs show so far

  • LeadLosing runs reach the act-2 boss with less HP (about 74% vs 90% for runs that win), and many non-boss losses start the fight at 11–30% HP (Snake Plant, Large Slime, Sentry and Sphere). The HP is lost in the fights on the way, not chosen on the map (next item).
  • Not a bugMap choices at low HP (below 40%): when a rest site is on offer the agent takes it — 44 of 48 times in losing runs, 12 of 14 in winning runs — and it picks a fight over an event, shop or treasure room about as often in losses as in wins (21% vs 29%).
  • LeadBoss fights where blocking looked better than racing: in the Automaton loss (seed 1366518) the agent attacks with 34 damage incoming and two Shrug It Off in hand, and never targets the orbs. The search budget matters here: twice the simulations wins about 4.6 points more on A10 (F39). Whether more search changes this exact turn can be checked directly.
  • OpenRich runs that skip shops late: one run met the act-3 boss with 466 gold unspent because its act-3 path had no shop. Winning runs spent about twice as much at shops; part of that is that they live longer.
  • OpenPotions spent early in boss fights: four potions on turn 1 against the Collector (seed 1366510), before the boss attacked.
  • Not a bugFree (0-cost) block cards left unplayed while taking damage: every case checked came from Corruption, where playing a skill exhausts it. Keeping the card is a real trade-off.
  • Not a bugRest sites at full HP that do nothing: the run held Fusion Hammer, which forbids upgrading.
