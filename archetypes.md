# Ironclad — Archetypes

Decks rarely fit one archetype cleanly; they layer two or three. Use this page
to (a) name the shape your deck is taking, and (b) decide whether a card
on offer is a payoff, an enabler, or a tax.

For each archetype: **payoffs** are the win-condition cards (need at least one),
**enablers** make the payoff work (want several), **taxes** are cards you take
*because* you went this route even though they'd be filler otherwise.

---

## 1. Strength

Stack +Str so every Attack hit (and every multi-hit) deals more.

- **Payoffs.** Demon Form, Limit Break, Heavy Blade, Whirlwind, Reaper, Sword Boomerang, Bludgeon
- **Enablers.** Inflame, Spot Weakness, Flex, Vajra, Girya, Du-Vu Doll
- **Bash** keeps Vulnerable up so Strength translates into a bigger %.
- **Synergy with Exhaust.** Limit Break + Corruption + Feel No Pain.
- **Watch out.** Strength alone doesn't stop damage — pair with one block plan.

## 2. Block / Defence ("Barricade / Body Slam")

Make Block persistent, then turn it into damage or just outlast everything.

- **Payoffs.** Barricade, Body Slam, Juggernaut, Entrench, Metallicize
- **Enablers.** Iron Wave, Shrug It Off, True Grit, Impervious, Ghostly Armor, Plated Armor cards
- **Relics.** Calipers (keeps Block between turns), Torii, Tungsten Rod, Thread and Needle
- **Body Slam** + Barricade is the canonical 0-cost nuke.
- **Watch out.** Without an Energy/draw plan, Barricade gets stuck in hand.

## 3. Exhaust

Use cards once for big effect, then trigger payoffs every Exhaust.

- **Payoffs.** Feel No Pain, Dark Embrace, Corruption, Sentinel, Second Wind, Fiend Fire
- **Enablers.** Impervious, Headbutt, Shrug It Off, True Grit, all Exhaust-on-play cards (Reaper, Offering, Immolate, Dropkick)
- **Relics.** Dead Branch (★ insane with Corruption), Charon's Ashes, Strange Spoon
- **Corruption + Dead Branch** is one of the strongest combos in the game.
- **Watch out.** Don't gut your deck — keep at least one reliable damage card.

## 4. Strike-tribal

Perfected Strike scales off every card with "Strike" in the name.

- **Payoff.** Perfected Strike (and to a lesser extent, Pommel Strike for draw)
- **Enablers.** Twin Strike, Wild Strike, Pommel Strike, Sneaky/Swift/Meteor Strike, Strike Dummy relic
- **Don't remove your basic Strikes.** Upgrade Perfected Strike early.
- **Watch out.** Wild Strike pollutes with Wounds — turn that into a feature with Wound-scaling (see #5).

## 5. Wound / Pollute scaling ("Rupture")

Embrace self-damage; every HP loss triggers a payoff.

- **Payoffs.** Rupture, Brutality, Combust (kind of)
- **Enablers.** Hemokinesis, Bloodletting, Reckless Charge, Wild Strike, Offering, Pummel cost trick
- **Relics.** Runic Cube, Self-Forming Clay, Centennial Puzzle
- **Watch out.** You need a backup heal (Burning Blood, Bird-Faced Urn from Powers, Feed kills).

## 6. Powers-matter

Spam Powers; each one triggers extra value.

- **Enablers / payoffs.** Bird-Faced Urn (heal 2), Mummified Hand (free random card), Bottled Tornado, Inserter
- **Anything with type=power.** Ironclad has 14: Berserk, Brutality, Combust, Corruption, Dark Embrace, Demon Form, Evolve, Feel No Pain, Fire Breathing, Inflame, Juggernaut, Metallicize, Rupture, Sadistic Nature.
- **Note.** Most builds want 2-3 powers max; this archetype wants 4+ and a way to play them (Mummified Hand, Enchiridion).

## 7. Whirlwind / Big-energy

Generate Energy, dump it into X-cost AoE.

- **Payoffs.** Whirlwind, Immolate (just for cost-efficiency)
- **Enablers.** Berserk, Bloodletting, Seeing Red, Brutality, Coffee Dripper / Sozu / any Energy relic
- **Relic.** Chemical X (X-cost cards behave as if X+2)
- **Whirlwind + Vulnerable + Strength** is a classic boss-killer turn.

## 8. Burst / 1-shot

Set up a single huge turn that kills the boss.

- **Payoffs.** Bludgeon (single-target nuke), Heavy Blade with Strength, Reaper for AoE+heal
- **Enablers.** Headbutt (re-use), Havoc / Exhume to recycle, Necronomicon (replays cost-2+ Attacks)
- **Watch out.** If you fail the kill, you usually die — have a back-up turn.

## 9. Searing Blow / minimum deck

Remove every card you can; keep upgrading Searing Blow.

- **Payoff.** Searing Blow (scales linearly with upgrades)
- **Enablers.** Card removal (Peace Pipe, Empty Cage, shop removals), Apotheosis-via-event
- **Watch out.** Very greedy, fragile, only works if you find heavy removal early.

## How to spot an archetype mid-run

After every card pick, ask: *what direction has this deck committed to?*

- Heavy Blade or Demon Form picked? → lean Strength.
- Barricade or Body Slam picked? → lean Block.
- Corruption or Dark Embrace picked? → lean Exhaust.
- 3+ "Strike" cards in hand often? → lean Strike-tribal.
- Lots of Wound generators? → either remove them or pivot to Rupture.

A deck without an archetype is a pile of B-tier cards; pick the
archetype-defining card *first*, then build around it.

## Boss-relic checklist (Ironclad)

Energy-up boss relics (most boss relics for Ironclad add +1 Energy with a cost):

| Relic | When to take | When to skip |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee Dripper | Always solid; you want the energy | If you have 0 healing/HP plan |
| Ectoplasm | Heavy-shop runs (no gold needed?) | Most runs — gold matters |
| Cursed Key | Have removal, or have Du-Vu Doll | Tiny decks where curse hurts |
| Fusion Hammer | Already upgraded most cards | Early act 1 boss |
| Mark of Pain | Have Exhaust / Wound payoffs | Otherwise — 2 dead cards every fight |
| Philosopher's Stone | Strong with a +energy plan | Enemies get Str (ouch vs. multi-hit) |
| Runic Dome | You've memorised intents | Otherwise huge information loss |
| Sozu | You don't use potions much | Most runs use potions a lot |
| Velvet Choker | You play ≤6 cards/turn anyway | Draw decks (Battle Trance, Pommel) |

Non-energy boss-relics worth always considering: **Pandora's Box** (deck reset),
**Snecko Eye** (best Ironclad relic when you have any draw), **Astrolabe**
(transform 3 + upgrade), **Sacred Bark** (potions), **Black Star** (more elite relics),
**Tiny House** (utility lump).
