## Tier S

### Seek `skill` `rare` `cost 0` `tier S`

_tags_: draw, exhaust

**Effect.** Put 1 card from your draw pile into your hand. Exhaust.

**Upgrade.** Put 2 cards from your draw pile into your hand. Exhaust.

**Notes.** Seek is a decent way of improving the consistency of your deck. When upgraded, it allows you to pull any two cards from your draw pile. With two upgraded copies of seek, you can draw any three cards from your draw pile by seeking your seek. This makes putting combo pieces together a breeze, such as a huge double energy turn with an X cost card. I would always pick up seek over sketching, but sometimes there are better rare cards available. Synergises exceptionally well with bottled lightning.

**★ Super-synergies.** Double Energy, Seek, Bottled Lightning

**Synergies.** Panache, Chrysalis, Metamorphosis, All For One, Scrape, Skim, Sunder, Beta, Happy Flower, Letter Opener, Toxic Egg, Dead Branch, Frozen Eye, Sundial, Unceasing Top, The Abacus

**Anti-synergies.** Normality, Regret, Aggregate, Snecko Eye, Velvet Choker

## Tier A+

### Echo Form `power` `rare` `cost 3` `tier A+`

_tags_: ethereal

**Effect.** The first card you play each turn is played twice. Ethereal.

**Upgrade.** The first card you play each turn is played twice.

**Notes.** Insanely powerful, and a common card to end a winning run with. With two Echo Forms, by proccing Echo Form on Echo Form you can get 3 Echo Forms in play. This demolishes everything except the Time Eater. Only downsides are how expensive it is and the fact that it takes a turn to start working. If you see an echo form, pick it. If you see a second echo form pick that too.

## Tier A

### Biased Cognition `power` `rare` `cost 1` `tier A`

_tags_: focus

**Effect.** Gain 4 Focus. At the start of each turn, lose 1 Focus.

**Upgrade.** Gain 5 Focus. At the start of each turn, lose 1 Focus.

**Notes.** Biased cognition is single-handedly the most powerful defect card for hallway fights. It is extremely fast acting, cheap, and has no downsides until turn 5. It is comparable to Wraith Form: you do not want to play this until you are sure that combat will be over before its effect wears off. This makes it a bit of a gamble in boss fights, but its benefits greatly outweigh its downsides. I would always recommend picking up at least one copy of this card. If you have any ways of getting artifact, the downside of this card can be fully negated making it incredibly powerful. Furthermore, orange pellets can remove the focus down debuff, and since this power is so cheap it is very easy to consistently pull this off on the turn that it is played. 

**★ Super-synergies.** Core Surge, Orange Pellets

**Synergies (41).** Panacea, Amplify, Ball Lightning, Biased Cognition, Capacitor, Chaos, Chill, Cold Snap, Consume, Coolheaded, Darkness, Defragment, Doom and Gloom, Dualcast, Fission, Force Field, Glacier, Heatsinks, Meteor Strike, Multi-Cast, Rainbow, Recursion, Static Discharge, Storm, Tempest, …

### Hologram `skill` `common` `cost 1` `tier B+`

_tags_: dblock, shuffle

**Effect.** Gain 3 Block. Return a card from your discard pile to your hand. Exhaust.

**Upgrade.** Gain 5 Block. Return a card from your discard pile to your hand.

**Notes.** Hologram is an extremely powerful common card for the defect, although at first glance it looks fairly insignificant. I would happily take up to three or four holograms in my deck. Being able to pull any card out of your discard pile is incredibly powerful and makes piecing combo pieces together much more consistent. It is adaptive, allowing you to pull either defensive or offensive cards depending on what is required. Even without providing any block, this card would still be powerful. The defect has a crazy combo, where you can use two holograms and two copies of madness to make the holograms free and then generate infinite block by having the holograms pull each other out. This works very well against the heart or with relics such as letter opener.

### Reboot `skill` `rare` `cost 0` `tier B+`

_tags_: shuffle, draw, exhaust

**Effect.** Shuffle all of your cards into your draw pile, then draw 4 cards. Exhaust.

**Upgrade.** Shuffle all of your cards into your draw pile, then draw 6 cards. Exhaust.

**Notes.** The biggest upside for reboot is that it can always give you a new hand if your current cards are not sufficient. This means that I would always rather pick up a reboot than skipping a card completely. That said, a lot of the other rares are often better choices. Because this card shuffles your deck, it triggers relics that benefit from shuffling. As a side note, reboot helps you put good cards back into your draw pile. If you have already played the best half of your deck, reshuffling your draw pile increases the likelihood of you drawing these strong cards again. Conversely if you have played the worst half of your deck, it might be worth not reshuffling. this is especially relevant if you are waiting for a strong power that is still at the bottom of your deck. Because this card empties your discard pile, it has antisynergy with hologram.

**Synergies.** Panache, Chrysalis, Metamorphosis, All For One, Scrape, Skim, Streamline, Beta, Happy Flower, Letter Opener, Toxic Egg, Dead Branch, Frozen Eye, Sundial, Unceasing Top, The Abacus

**Anti-synergies.** Normality, Regret, Aggregate, Hologram, Snecko Eye, Velvet Choker

## Tier B

### Creative AI `power` `rare` `cost 3→2` `tier B`

_tags_: create

**Effect.** At the start of each turn, add a random Power card to your hand.

**Notes.** Creative AI is a win condition and fits into the power archetype the defect has. Many of the defect’s powers trigger when playing other powers, such as storm, so this tends to spiral out of control quickly. If your deck is lacking any sort of direction or win condition, creative AI can come in handy. It obviously synergises very well with amplify. This card’s downside is that it is very slow and also very expensive so in hallway fights you might find yourself not able to play it. However, just like demon form, it can single-handedly turn the tide in boss battles. Note that this archetype is very heavily countered by the awakened, so consider playing slowly and holding back on this power until the awakened reaches phase 2. 

**★ Super-synergies.** Amplify, Force Field, Heatsinks, Storm

**Synergies.** Mind Blast, Frozen Egg, Mummified Hand, Bird-Faced Urn, Gambling Chip, Snecko Eye, Pocketwatch

**Anti-synergies.** Regret, Unceasing Top, Velvet Choker
