# Potions (Ironclad-relevant)

> **Source note:** Potions are NOT in the spirespy scrape that the rest of
> this KB derives from. This file is hand-curated from established vanilla
> Slay the Spire potion text. If a potion appears on-screen whose name is
> not in this file, **don't invent an entry** — cite it as
> `(on-screen, no KB entry)` and copy the in-game tooltip into the recap.
> Add a verified entry here afterward if it'll recur.

Potions cost 0 energy, are used outside the normal play action, and persist
their effect for the duration noted (turn / combat / instant). Ironclad
starts with **3 potion slots** (slot count can grow via Potion Belt /
Cauldron / etc.).

---

## Class-agnostic potions (any character can drop them)

### Attack Potion
**Effect.** Choose 1 of 3 random Attack cards to add to your hand. The card
costs 0 this turn.

### Skill Potion
**Effect.** Choose 1 of 3 random Skill cards to add to your hand. Costs 0
this turn.

### Power Potion
**Effect.** Choose 1 of 3 random Power cards to add to your hand. Costs 0
this turn. **Ironclad note:** can fish for Demon Form / Inflame / Limit
Break — strong on bosses if you're not already holding a Power.

### Block Potion
**Effect.** Gain 12 Block.

### Energy Potion
**Effect.** Gain 2 Energy this turn. **Ironclad note:** S-tier — enables
multi-card combo turns (Bash + Pommel Strike + scaler).

### Strength Potion
**Effect.** Gain 2 Strength (lasts the combat).

### Flex Potion
**Effect.** Gain 5 Strength. At the end of combat, lose 5 Strength.
**Ironclad note:** Use the same turn as Whirlwind / Heavy Blade /
multi-hit for burst.

### Dexterity Potion
**Effect.** Gain 2 Dexterity (more Block per Block-granting card, this
combat). **Ironclad note:** weaker for us than for Defect / Watcher
because we have few Dex-scaling cards.

### Fire Potion
**Effect.** Deal 20 damage to a single enemy.

### Explosive Potion
**Effect.** Deal 10 damage to ALL enemies.

### Fear Potion
**Effect.** Apply 3 Vulnerable to a single enemy. **Ironclad note:**
combos hard with Bag of Marbles, Bash, Paper Phrog.

### Weak Potion
**Effect.** Apply 3 Weak to a single enemy. Reduces their attack damage 25%.

### Smoke Bomb
**Effect.** Escape from any non-boss combat (no rewards, no gold). Cannot
be used in boss fights.

### Snecko Oil
**Effect.** Draw 5 cards. For the rest of this combat, every card you draw
has a random cost 0–3.

### Fruit Juice
**Effect.** Gain 5 Max HP (immediate, permanent).

### Liquid Bronze
**Effect.** Gain 3 Thorns (combat).

### Liquid Memories
**Effect.** Choose 1 card in your discard pile. It returns to your hand
and costs 0 this turn.

### Essence of Steel
**Effect.** Gain 4 Plated Armor (combat). Plated Armor blocks like Block
but only decays when you take unblocked damage.

### Ghost in a Jar
**Effect.** Gain 1 Intangible (1 turn). All incoming damage of any kind
reduced to 1 that turn.

### Duplication Potion
**Effect.** This turn, your next card is played twice.

### Ancient Potion
**Effect.** Gain 1 Artifact (combat). Negates the next debuff applied to
you.

### Cultist Potion
**Effect.** Gain 1 Ritual (combat). Gain 1 Strength at the end of every turn.

### Regen Potion
**Effect.** Gain 5 Regeneration. Heal 5 HP at end of next turn, then 4, 3,
2, 1. Total heal 15.

### Distilled Chaos
**Effect.** Play the top 3 cards of your draw pile.

### Gambler's Brew
**Effect.** Discard any number of cards from hand, then draw that many.

### Entropic Brew
**Effect.** Fill all empty potion slots with random potions.

---

## Ironclad-only potions

### Blood Potion
**Effect.** Heal HP equal to 20% of your Max HP. **Note:** S-tier for
Ironclad — only class-locked heal-on-demand we have.

### Elixir
**Effect.** Exhaust any number of cards from your hand. **Note:** key for
Exhaust archetype (Corruption / Dark Embrace / Feel No Pain payoffs);
also clears Wounds, Burns, Slimed, Dazed.
