# Silent — Archetypes

Decks rarely fit one archetype cleanly; they layer two or three. Use this page
to (a) name the shape your deck is taking, and (b) decide whether a card
on offer is a payoff, an enabler, or a tax.

For each archetype: **payoffs** are the win-condition cards (need at least one),
**enablers** make the payoff work (want several), **taxes** are cards you take
*because* you went this route even though they'd be filler otherwise.

> Cross-reference with `silent_cards_by_tag.md` for "all poison cards / all
> discard cards / all shiv cards" lookups, and `silent_cards.md` for full
> tier and synergy notes.

## 1. Poison

"Maintain block while ramping up lethal amounts of Poison." Stall enemies,
let poison damage tick, finish with a Catalyst multiplier.

- **Payoffs.** Catalyst (×2 / ×3 poison), Nightmare (triple a key card),
  Burst (replay a skill — usually Catalyst), Corpse Explosion (AoE chain).
- **Enablers.** Noxious Fumes (★ best ramp), Bouncing Flask, Crippling Cloud,
  Deadly Poison, Poisoned Stab, Envenom (passive ramp).
- **Block plan.** Footwork, Leg Sweep, Backflip, Dodge and Roll,
  Well-Laid Plans (holds Catalyst safe).
- **Relics.** **Snecko Skull (S+)** — +1 poison every application, single
  most important relic; **The Specimen** — transfers poison from dying
  enemies, turns single-target poison into AoE. Sundial / Snecko Eye / any
  draw helps you find Catalyst.
- **Antisynergies.** Bane (looks like a payoff but bloats the deck), Artifact
  enemies (Sentries, Spheric Guardian) eat your first poison stack — bring
  Bouncing Flask or Noxious Fumes to burn through.
- **Watch out.** Time Eater, The Champ, Awakened One all clear debuffs and
  hard-counter pure poison. Reptomancer scales faster than your poison if
  you don't have Catalyst by act 3.
- **Tip.** In high-Asc, "Catalyst can be hard to find." If you don't have it
  by end of act 2, pivot toward Envenom Poison or pick up Burst as a backup
  multiplier.

## 2. Envenom Poison

A hybrid: take **Envenom** (whenever an attack deals unblocked damage,
apply 1 Poison), then attack a lot. Less Catalyst-dependent than pure poison.

- **Payoffs.** Envenom (★ the whole archetype), Catalyst (multiplier), Bane
  (now actually good — 7 dmg + 7 more if Poisoned).
- **Enablers (multi-hit attacks distribute Envenom poison fast).**
  Sneaky Strike, Skewer, Riddle with Holes, Dagger Spray, Flying Knee,
  All-Out Attack, Choke, Finisher.
- **Relics.** Snecko Skull again. Pen Nib, Akabeko, Vajra, Ornamental Fan
  (any per-attack rider compounds well).
- **Why pivot here from pure Poison?** You found Envenom but no Catalyst,
  or you have a strong Shiv/multi-hit base — Envenom turns those attacks
  into ramping poison damage. Beats Time Eater / Awakened One better than
  pure poison because the poison reapplies every hit.
- **Watch out.** Envenom is rare (1 per draft pool), and the deck wants both
  attacks AND poison enablers — easy to get torn between two halves.

## 3. Shiv ("Death by a thousand cuts")

Generate huge volume of 0-cost Shiv attacks; scale with per-attack riders.

- **Payoffs.** A Thousand Cuts (★ chip-AoE per card), After Image (★ block
  per card), Accuracy (★ +4/+6 dmg per shiv — the multiplier card),
  Infinite Blades (Shiv every turn).
- **Enablers.** Blade Dance (★ 3 shivs for 1 energy), Cloak and Dagger,
  Storm of Steel, Dagger Throw, Sneaky Strike (1 energy back if no Attack
  played this turn — combos with Shiv-only turns).
- **Support.** Choke, Panache (colorless: per-card chip), Finisher (scales
  with attack count this turn).
- **Relics.** **Wrist Blade** (boss, +4 dmg to 0-cost attacks — the canonical
  Shiv boss relic), **Shuriken** (uncommon, +1 Str per 3 attacks),
  **Kunai** (uncommon, +1 Dex per 3 attacks), **Ornamental Fan** (4 block
  per 3 attacks), **Nunchaku** (energy per 10 attacks), **Vajra** (1 Str
  start of combat).
- **Antisynergies.** **Velvet Choker** (6-card limit gut-punches Shiv volume),
  **Strange Spoon** (sometimes wastes a shiv-on-discard), **Dead Branch**
  (random card pollutes the lean cycler).
- **Watch out.** Time Eater (12-card play limit) and the Heart hard-counter
  Shiv. Pack Piercing Wail / Crippling Cloud as defensive scaling.
- **Pivot trigger.** Picking up Shuriken or Kunai is the cleanest signal.
  Early Blade Dance + Accuracy on offer = lock in.

## 4. Discard

Discarding is normally a downside — turn it into a payoff trigger.

- **Payoffs (cards that gain when discarded).** Tactician (+1 energy when
  discarded), Reflex (+2 cards when discarded). Hold both with Well-Laid
  Plans so you always have something to discard.
- **Enablers (force discards).** Calculated Gamble (★ discard hand, draw
  hand back), Tools of the Trade (★ each turn: draw 1, discard 1),
  Acrobatics (draw 3, discard 1), Survivor (8 block + discard 1),
  Dagger Throw, Prepared, Eviscerate (cheaper per discard this turn).
- **Damage payoff.** Finisher, Flechettes (per Skill in hand), Shiv if
  you're hybriding.
- **Relics.** **Tough Bandages** (uncommon, ★ +3 block per discard) and
  **Tingsha** (rare, ★ 3 dmg to random enemy per discard) — the two
  "discard = free value" relics. Letter Opener, Hovering Kite (boss, 1
  energy on first discard each turn).
- **Watch out.** Damage output is low without Finisher / Flechettes / Shiv
  hybrid. Time Eater and the Heart still hurt. Pack Piercing Wail.
- **Pivot trigger.** Tough Bandages or Tingsha drop = commit. Tactician /
  Reflex without a discard relic is a brick deck.

## 5. Infinite Discard

A specialised discard build that loops itself for unlimited damage / block.

- **Core combo.** Tools of the Trade (always discard, always draw) +
  Tactician × N (each discard gives +1 energy) + Reflex × N (each
  discard draws 2 more) → infinite cycle if your deck is thin enough.
- **Payoff.** Bouncing Flask (poison ramps every cycle), A Thousand Cuts,
  Tingsha (chip per discard), Envenom + any attack.
- **Enablers.** Heavy card removal (event + shop), Snecko Skull / Sozu / Egg
  to thin further. Adrenaline / Setup as kickoff.
- **Relics.** Tingsha + Tough Bandages double as damage and block.
  **Dead Branch** here is great — every Tactician discard is also a free
  random card.
- **Watch out.** Time Eater (12-card limit ends the loop), Velvet Choker,
  Ascender's Bane (if drawn into the cycle, no infinite).
- **Don't force this.** It needs ~3 specific cards + a relic. If you don't
  have the pieces by act 2, fall back to plain Discard.

## 6. Grand Finale

A single-card archetype: empty your draw pile, slam Grand Finale (50 dmg AoE).

- **Payoff.** Grand Finale (★ 0-cost: only playable with empty draw pile;
  60 dmg upgraded). Rare — find it before committing.
- **Enablers (cycle the draw pile fast).** Setup, Bullet Time (all cards
  cost 0 this turn), Tools of the Trade, Acrobatics, Calculated Gamble,
  Adrenaline, Concentrate (discard 3 → +2 energy), Backflip, Predator.
- **Relics.** **Runic Pyramid** (★ keep your hand — store Grand Finale
  while you cycle; same idea as Well-Laid Plans), **Hovering Kite**
  (energy from discards — fuels the cycle), **Snecko Eye** (variance but
  gives you draw + cost shenanigans).
- **Watch out.** If you can't reliably empty the draw pile turn 2-3, this
  archetype is dead. Needs a thin deck (≤16 cards) or a draw engine.
- **Hybrids well with.** Discard (you're discarding to empty draw anyway),
  Shiv (shivs are 0-cost, count as plays).

## 7. Defensive scaling (sub-archetype, not standalone)

Silent's defensive scalers slot into any of the above as a survival floor.

- **Footwork** (A): +2/+3 Dex per cast — every block card scales.
- **Wraith Form** (S): 2 Intangible turns; ramps Dex loss but trivializes
  any boss for those turns.
- **After Image** (A+): block per card played — auto-includes in any
  high-tempo deck (Shiv, Discard).
- **Calipers / Tungsten Rod / Torii** (relics) all extend block plans.
- **Use case.** Take 1-2 of these as a "sub-archetype" alongside your real
  win condition. Silent doesn't have an Ironclad-style "block-payoff"
  archetype (no Body Slam equivalent), so block is always supportive.

## How to spot an archetype mid-run

After every card pick, ask: *what direction has this deck committed to?*

- Catalyst, Noxious Fumes, or Snecko Skull picked? → **Poison.**
- Envenom + multi-hit attacks? → **Envenom Poison.**
- 2+ Shiv generators or Wrist Blade / Shuriken / Kunai? → **Shiv.**
- Tools of the Trade + Tough Bandages or Tingsha? → **Discard.**
- Grand Finale offered + thin deck plan? → **Grand Finale.**
- Tactician + Reflex + Tools + Dead Branch? → **Infinite Discard** (rare).

A deck without an archetype is a pile of B-tier cards. Pick the
archetype-defining card *first*, then draft around it. Silent has more
"obvious" payoff cards than Ironclad — don't skip them when offered.

## Boss-relic checklist (Silent)

Silent's energy/boss relics, with archetype context:

| Relic | When to take | When to skip |
|---|---|---|
| **Wrist Blade** | Shiv build (★ canonical) | Non-Shiv decks — 4 dmg/0-cost is niche |
| **Hovering Kite** | Discard / Infinite Discard / Grand Finale | Low-discard decks |
| **Ring of the Serpent** | Always solid — extra draw turn 1 | Almost never a skip |
| **Coffee Dripper** | Aggressive decks; you skip rests anyway | If you're low HP or need healing |
| **Ectoplasm** | Heavy-deck run, no shop reliance | Most runs — gold matters for Smith / removal |
| **Cursed Key** | Have card removal, or have Du-Vu Doll | Tiny decks where curse hurts more |
| **Fusion Hammer** | Most cards already upgraded | Pre-act 1 boss — skip Smiths feels bad |
| **Philosopher's Stone** | +energy plan; multi-hit attacks | Enemies-get-Str hurts Shiv decks badly |
| **Runic Dome** | You've memorised intents | Otherwise large information loss |
| **Sozu** | You hate potions | Silent uses potions A LOT — usually skip |
| **Velvet Choker** | You play ≤6 cards/turn (slow Poison ramp) | **Skip** for Shiv / Discard / any combo deck |
| **Snecko Eye** | Always strong; Silent already wants draw | You need precise costs (Catalyst hand) |
| **Pandora's Box** | Want to nuke the basic deck | Already have a plan and curated picks |
| **Astrolabe** | Pre-act 2 (more upside time) | Late act 3 (less time to leverage) |
| **Sacred Bark** | Potion-heavy run | Already have Sozu / no potions |
| **Black Star** | Want to multiply elite drops (more relics) | Late run; act 3 elites are scariest |
| **Tiny House** | Utility lump (HP + gold + card + upgrade) | Almost always a fine pick |
| **Calling Bell** | Earlyish (3 relic slots help) | Late act 3 |
| **Busted Crown** | Already have fat scalers; +1 energy outweighs single-card rewards | Early — fewer card rewards hurts archetype-finding |

Non-Silent-specific relics worth always considering for Silent:
**Pen Nib** (every 10th attack ×2 — incredible with Shiv volume),
**Shuriken** / **Kunai** (per-3-attacks Str/Dex — Shiv staples),
**Snecko Skull** (★ poison amplifier — S+ tier),
**The Specimen** (poison transfer — A- staple in Poison),
**Tough Bandages** + **Tingsha** (Discard staples),
**Twisted Funnel** (Weak start of combat — slots into any deck),
**Ninja Scroll** (3 Shivs in opening hand — Shiv kickstart),
**Paper Krane** (Weak does -75% instead of -25%),
**Tingsha** (★ Discard; per-discard random damage),
**Tough Bandages** (★ Discard; per-discard 3 block).
